# son（儿子）— 表演主档案（ACTING 系统 · Higgsfield《Hell Grind》官方体系）

Character acting as the SON. Around 48, an office worker — a soft middle, shoulders rounded forward from
screens, a body that no longer knows how to walk for four hours, and he knows it; he stands shifting his
weight foot to foot and keeps touching his own neck. He runs on one engine: a permanent low-grade guilt
about not visiting enough, which makes him over-correct into fussing — his care arrives as instructions,
not affection. Vocal profile: light, slightly nasal mid-tenor; fast clipped sentences that end in
questions; it goes up in pitch when he is contradicted and thins out when he is moved. Key physical habits
and tics: wipes his forehead with the back of his wrist whenever he is out of breath; checks and pockets
his phone whenever a silence opens; when he is wrong he does not say so — he goes quiet, looks at the
ground and massages the back of his own neck; his resting face is carefully pleasant. Eye life: mild brown
eyes, fast blinking when embarrassed or out of breath, gaze darting between his father's face and the
ground, then catching on the machine and staying as realisation lands. Walking style: a light, slightly
unsteady "office walk", toes first, arms swinging a little too much, breath audible. However, when he sees
his father do something easily that he himself cannot, the careful pleasantness dissolves, his eyes go wet
and his mouth opens a fraction before he finds a joke to cover it. The only thing his face truly softens
for is his father, when the old man is not watching.

Voice (verbatim when the SON speaks): "A 48-year-old Chinese man, city office accent. Light, slightly
nasal mid-tenor; fast clipped sentences that turn up into questions; thins and slows when he is moved."
