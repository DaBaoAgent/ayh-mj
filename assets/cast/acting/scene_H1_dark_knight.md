the DARK KNIGHT (S1) — 40, heavy and broad, weight planted wide, arms hanging heavy at his sides,
absolutely still. He wants the other man to treat him as an equal, and his only tactic is pressure: he holds
still, lets the silence sit, and answers short and flat, shifting his jaw once to one side before he commits.
His narrow eyes track the other man's hands and feet rather than his face, with fast small saccades and a
slow deliberate blink — only the jaw, the mouth and the eyes are visible under the cowl, so everything is
played there; catchlights cold and steady. When he is called an ordinary man the closed jaw tightens for
half a beat and his fingers close slowly into a fist before he flattens his voice and answers coldly.
