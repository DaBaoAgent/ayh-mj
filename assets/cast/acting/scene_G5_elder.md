WANG (S2) — 70, thick-set, weight settled wide, back straight, one hand on the control. He wants to be
proved capable, not cared for: when the son slows him down his jaw sets and he answers fast and flat; once
decided he taps two fingers twice on the armrest. His eyes flick to the son's face to check the fussing has
stopped, then to the machine — quick saccades, a slow deliberate blink, live catchlights, never a frozen stare.
When the son calls him fragile the wry half-smile vanishes for half a beat and real hurt flickers through
before a dry laugh buries it.
