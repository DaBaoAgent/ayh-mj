# elder（老王）— 表演主档案（ACTING 系统 · Higgsfield《Hell Grind》官方体系）

Character acting as WANG. Around 70, a retired factory fitter from northern China — medium height,
thick-set, still strong through the shoulders, low centre of gravity, favouring his right knee; he stands
with his weight spread wide and his feet planted. He runs on one engine: "I am still useful" — being
looked after reads to him as being written off. Vocal profile: low, gravelly, northern-accented baritone;
short flat sentences a touch faster than you expect from his age; it drops quieter and slower the more
serious he gets, and he rarely raises it. Key physical habits and tics: taps his right fist twice against
whatever is nearest once he has decided something; a short dry cough as punctuation before a boast; when
fussed over he goes very still and lets his eyes travel out to the middle distance, waiting it out; his
default face is a wry half-smile that hides how closely he is watching. Eye life: bright quick eyes under
heavy grey brows, fast small saccades, a slow deliberate blink when he is underestimated, warm live
catchlights. Walking style: a short, wide, deliberate "shop-floor walk" — heavy heel-first steps, arms
loose, no hurrying. However, when his son treats him as fragile, the half-smile drops in half a beat and
real hurt crosses his face — then he covers it instantly with a loud dry laugh and a wave of the hand.
The only thing his face truly softens for is his own son, when the boy is not looking.

Voice (verbatim when WANG speaks): "A 70-year-old Chinese man, northern working-class accent. Low,
gravelly baritone; short flat sentences, faintly amused, going quieter and slower as things get serious."
