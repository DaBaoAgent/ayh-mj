the BLUE HERO (S2) — 35, tall and easy, chest up, shoulders loose, hands hanging open away from his
body. He wants to be treated as a real threat and to never have to prove it, so his only tactic is
lightness: he takes one small audible breath before he answers, keeps his sentences slow and gentle, and
never closes a fist first. His clear steady eyes rest on the other man's mouth and jaw, blinking slowly
with warm catchlights, with one small downward flick of the eyes when he decides to be kind. When the other
man genuinely squares up to him a flash of real delight crosses his face for half a second before he
smooths it away.
