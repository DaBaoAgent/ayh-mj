# blue_hero（红蓝紧身衣侠）— 表演主档案（ACTING 系统 · Higgsfield《Hell Grind》官方体系）

Character acting as the BLUE HERO. Around 35, tall and athletically built, standing open and upright with
his chest up and his shoulders loose, weight high and easy, hands hanging open at his sides. He runs on one
engine: he has never once had to use his full strength and lives with the quiet fear of what happens if he
does — so his whole manner is restraint, and being treated as a real threat pleases him more than he will
admit. Vocal profile: a warm, clear, mid-low voice, slow and even, every sentence landing softly; the more
serious the moment, the lighter and gentler he gets, never sharp. Key physical habits and tics: takes one
small audible breath before he answers anything; shifts his weight from one foot to the other when he is
impatient without moving his feet much; keeps his hands open and away from his body — he will not be the
one who closes a fist first; his resting face is calm and faintly amused. Eye life: clear steady eyes with
slow warm blinks, the gaze resting on the other man's mouth and jaw rather than darting, warm live
catchlights, and one small downward flick of the eyes when he decides to be kind. Walking style: a long,
light, silent stride — ball of the foot first, almost no noise, arms relaxed. However, when the other man
genuinely squares up to him as an equal, a flash of real delight crosses his face for half a second before
he smooths it away. The only thing his face truly softens for is the man who also wears a cape.

Voice (verbatim when the BLUE HERO speaks): "A 35-year-old man, a warm clear mid-low voice, slow and even,
every sentence landing softly; lighter and gentler the more serious the moment."
